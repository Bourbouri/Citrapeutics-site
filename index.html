First-in-Class ACOD1 Inhibitors

Targeting Immunometabolism for Next-Generation Cancer Therapy

Citrapeutics™ is developing oral small-molecule inhibitors targeting ACOD1 (IRG1), a key immunometabolic enzyme which drives immune suppression in tumour tissue. Our platform aims to restore anti-tumor immunity across multiple solid tumor indications.

Scientific Platform

ACOD1 is a metabolic enzyme expressed in activated myeloid immune cells (macrophages, dendritic cells, granulocytes) that that synthesizes the immunosuppressive metabolite itaconate.

Elevated ACOD1 activity has been associated with impaired T-cell function and tumor immune evasion.

Citrapeutics™ is advancing a pipeline of selective, orally bioavailable small molecules designed to inhibit ACOD1 and reprogram the tumor microenvironment. Our therapeutics are being developed for use as a monotherapy and potentially as combination therapy to achieve durable anti-tumor immunity.
