Unlocking ACOD1 for Cancer Immunotherapy

Citrapeutics is a preclinical-stage biotech venture developing novel, oral small-molecule inhibitors of ACOD1 (IRG1) to dismantle the immunosuppressive tumor microenvironment and restore anti-tumor immunity.

The Science

ACOD1 (Aconitate Decarboxylase 1) is an enzyme highly expressed by tumor-associated myeloid cells. It catalyzes the production of itaconic acid, a key metabolic suppressor that paralyzes local T-cells and shields the tumor from immune attacks.

By selectively blocking ACOD1, our small molecules reprogram these myeloid cells from an immunosuppressive state into a pro-inflammatory state. This shift effectively re-arms the patient's own immune system to target and destroy the cancer.

Our therapeutic platform is uniquely indication-agnostic: ACOD1 serves both as the target and a direct predictive biomarker. We are prioritizing solid tumor patients displaying high tissue expression or elevated systemic levels of itaconic acid.

Primary high-expression tumor indications include:

Breast Cancer Head & Neck Cancer Stomach Cancer Lung Cancer Colon Cancer Skin Cancer
