First-on-Target First-in-Class ACOD1 Inhibitors

Targeting Immunometabolism for Next-Generation Cancer Therapy

Citrapeutics is developing orally available small-molecule inhibitors targeting ACOD1 (IRG1), a key immunometabolic enzyme which drives immune suppression in tumour tissue. Our platform aims to restore anti-tumor immunity across multiple solid tumor indications.

Scientific Platform

ACOD1 is a metabolic enzyme expressed in activated myeloid immune cells (macrophages, dendritic cells, granulocytes) that synthesizes the immunosuppressive metabolite itaconate.

Elevated ACOD1 activity has been associated with impaired T-cell function and tumor immune evasion.

Citrapeutics is advancing a pipeline of selective, orally bioavailable small molecules designed to inhibit ACOD1 and reprogram the tumor microenvironment. Our therapeutics are being developed for use as monotherapy and potentially as combination therapy to achieve durable anti-tumor immunity.

About Us

Our scientific origins trace back to the Helmholtz Centre for Infection Research (HZI), where pioneering work on immunometabolism and itaconate biology laid the groundwork for ACOD1 as a compelling oncology target.

Translational research that underpins Citrapeutics has been spearheaded by Prof. Dr. Dr. Frank Pessler's Research Group at HZI and TWINCORE (Centre for Experimental and Clinical Infection Research). Prof. Pessler's group has made seminal contributions to understanding the role of ACOD1-derived itaconate in immune regulation and disease — work that directly informs Citrapeutics' drug discovery strategy.

We are a preclinical-stage biotech committed to developing first-on-target, first-in-class, orally bioavailable small-molecule ACOD1 inhibitors with the potential to transform outcomes for patients with solid tumors.
